A home away from home

Linga Bhairavi Apartment has 24 single rooms, each with its own attached toilet and kitchen.

This is not a PG. Each room is rented to singles who can make it a home away from home. A maximum of two people can stay in a room.

Rooms, photos and videos

Rooms are located at the front, middle and back of the building. The corner rooms are slightly bigger and have an additional window.

Rent and bills

₹12,000 – ₹14,000 per month

Single room with kitchen and toilet. Corner rooms are slightly bigger and have an additional window.

Included in rent
Water bills and all repair costs during the tenancy, including plumbing, electrical and carpentry repairs.
Paid separately
Electricity. Each room has its own meter, and tenants pay for the units used as per the meter.

Deposit

2 months' rent

Refund
The deposit is returned at the end of the tenancy, after deductions for cleaning and painting.
Deduction
₹7,000 is deducted from the deposit for cleaning and painting.
Settlement
The remaining amount is settled within a week of vacating.

Room facilities

  • Attached toilet with geyser
  • Attached kitchen, semi-furnished, with a small kitchen cabinet
  • Bed (without mattress)
  • Study table
  • Two-door cupboard with mirror
  • Water inlet and outlet for a washing machine (tenants may install their own)
  • Power backup is provided inside the room

Wi-Fi is not provided. Tenants can arrange their own connection.

Shared facilities

Parking
Bike parking on the ground floor.
Lift
The building has a lift.
Terrace
Tenants can use the terrace for drying clothes and short walks.

Tenancy terms

Minimum stay
6 months. Rooms are not rented for shorter periods. Early exit is possible by mutual understanding between tenant and owner.
Notice period
Tenants can vacate within 30 days of giving notice.
Guests
₹100 per day for overnight guests. Tenants keep track of guest days and pay the charges along with that month's rent.
House rules
There are no specific restrictions. We expect tenants and their guests to stay calm and not disturb neighbours.
Agreement
One-time agreement charge of ₹900, plus stamp costs of ₹550.

Cleaning and repairs

After each tenancy, the room, toilet and kitchen are professionally cleaned. All repairs are completed before the next tenancy begins.

Frequently asked questions

What is the rent?

₹12,000 to ₹14,000 per month. Water and repairs are included; electricity is billed as per your room's meter.

How much is the deposit, and do I get it back?

Two months' rent. It is returned at the end of the tenancy after a ₹7,000 deduction for cleaning and painting, and settled within a week of vacating.

Do I need to pay anything besides rent?

Yes. Tenants pay the monthly electricity bill as per their room's meter, a one-time agreement charge of ₹900 (plus stamp costs of ₹550), and ₹100 per day for overnight guests.

Is there a meter for the room?

Yes. Each room has its own electricity meter.

What is in the room?

Attached kitchen (with cabinet) and toilet (with geyser), a bed without mattress, a study table and a two-door cupboard with mirror.

Is there parking and a lift?

Bike parking is on the ground floor, and the building has a lift.

Where is the nearest bus stop?

Thubarahalli, about 0.5 km away.

Are there any restrictions?

No specific restrictions. Please keep noise down and respect the neighbours.

Can I see photos and videos of the rooms?

Yes, see the Rooms section above, or ask on WhatsApp.

Location and contact

Address: 2nd Cross, Meenakshi Layout, opposite Sumadhura Lake Breeze Apartments, Varthur Road
